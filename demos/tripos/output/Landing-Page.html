The problem

Your trip lives in a confirmation email, a booking app, a PDF, a group chat and a screenshot you can’t find when the train is pulling out.

→

TripOS puts it on one board.

What we’re building.

Not another planner and not a booking site. An organizer before you go, a companion while you’re there, and one plan your whole group can see.

  1. Organize.

    Before you go · Organize

    Bring your bookings and plans in from wherever they live. TripOS sorts them into one timeline you can read in a glance.

  2. Companion.

    On the trip · Companion

    When a train is cancelled or a flight slips, you get the next move, not a scavenger hunt through your inbox.

  3. Together.

    With your group · Together

    Everyone sees the same plan, the same times and who still owes for the tickets. The group chat stops being the itinerary.

Built for the semester abroad.

  • Your first trips as an independent adult.
  • Weekend hops across Europe between classes.
  • A friend group that has to agree on all of it.
