Rules

  • Yellow is under 5% of any screen, except the one band per page.
  • One dark board panel behind the conversion.
  • Headlines in sentence case, ending with a period.
  • No em dashes. No stock phrases.
  • Motion: one curve, cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1). The split-flap runs only on the hero board.
  • Removed, do not reintroduce: purple gradients, emoji icons, icon-in-a-circle feature grids.
  • No made-up stats, quotes or testimonials. Brochure facts are sourced.
  • Board status colors (go, alert) live only inside the board.
  • The only radius is 6px.
