Brand Basics · For marketing & owners

Our look, on one page.

A quick, plain-language reference for anyone creating YohDev marketing — the colors, the two fonts, the buttons, and a short list of do's and don'ts. No code required.

01 · Colors

02 · Type

Two typefaces, always

Headlines in Poppins. Everything else in Roboto. That's the whole rule — don't introduce a third font.

03 · Buttons

Buttons

One filled teal button for the main action; one outlined button for the secondary. Both are fully rounded.

Start a project → See our work

Use the filled teal button once per screen — it's the single most important action. Everything else is the outlined style.

04 · Building blocks

What a YohDev page is made of

Most pages are the same handful of blocks, stacked. If you can name the block, you can brief it.

Header

Logo left, a few links, one teal button. Collapses to a menu on phones.

Hero

A short, bold headline, one sentence of support, and the main button.

Content sections

A label, a heading, and either text, a list, or a row of cards.

Cards

Small dark panels with a title and a line or two — used in rows.

Call to action

A centered prompt near the bottom that repeats the main button.

Footer

Logo, a copyright line, and a short row of links on a black band.

05 · Rules of thumb

A few simple rules

✓ Do

  • Keep backgrounds dark and text bright.
  • Use teal to highlight one thing at a time.
  • Give headlines room to breathe.
  • Write short, plain, confident sentences.

✕ Don't

  • Put teal text on a light background.
  • Add a third font or a new color.
  • Crowd two filled buttons together.
  • Use tiny, low-contrast grey text.
